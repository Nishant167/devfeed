# Code of Conduct

DevFeed is built by people collaborating on shared technical work. This document sets expectations for how we work together.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully, even in disagreement.
- Critique code, architecture, and ideas directly and honestly — critique people never.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements in a technical project are about tradeoffs, not intent.
- Give feedback that's specific and actionable, not just critical.
- Be receptive to feedback on your own work; defensiveness slows everyone down.

## Constructive technical disagreement

Disagreement over architecture, technology choices, or implementation approach is normal and expected — see `CONTRIBUTING.md` on how to raise architectural changes. Disagreements are resolved by evidence and reasoning, not seniority or volume. If a decision has already been made and documented in `DEVFEED.md`, propose a change to the document rather than relitigating it in an unrelated PR.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome comments related to personal characteristics.
- Discrimination on the basis of race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, nationality, or any other protected characteristic.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or project work.

## Reporting concerns

If someone's behavior in an issue, pull request, or other project space violates this code of conduct, report it privately to the repository maintainers rather than escalating publicly. Reports are handled with discretion.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official DevFeed communication channels.
